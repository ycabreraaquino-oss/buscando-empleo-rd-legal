Buscando Empleo RD

Vacantes reales de República Dominicana, todos los días en TikTok

Buscando Empleo RD is an automated channel on TikTok (@BuscandoEmpleoRD) that publishes short videos announcing real, currently open job positions across the Dominican Republic — with the position, company, location, salary when available, and how to apply.

How it works

  • Job openings are collected from public job-alert emails from established job boards (such as Computrabajo and LinkedIn job alerts).
  • Each listing goes through automated checks: required fields, trusted sender domain, scam-phrase screening, and duplicate detection — anything suspicious or incomplete is discarded before it ever becomes a video.
  • Listings that pass are turned into a short vertical video with the job details and a narrated summary, and published to the TikTok channel.
