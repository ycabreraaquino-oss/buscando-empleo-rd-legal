Reels Automation RD

Automated short-video clips, published to our own TikTok account

Reels Automation RD is an automated system that watches a live video broadcast, identifies short highlight moments as they happen, edits them into vertical short-form videos, and publishes them to a single TikTok account that we own and operate.

How it works

  • The system continuously analyzes a live video stream for moments of high audience interest (sudden spikes in audio energy, reactions, dialogue).
  • Each candidate moment is transcribed and reviewed automatically, producing a short title and caption for the clip.
  • The clip is rendered as a short vertical video (captions burned in) and published directly to our own TikTok account via the TikTok Content Posting API.

Scope

This integration publishes content only to the single TikTok account we own. It does not log in on behalf of other TikTok users, does not read or modify other users' content, and does not offer any public-facing login or posting feature.
