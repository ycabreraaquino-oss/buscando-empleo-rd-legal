Terms of Service — Buscando Empleo RD

Last updated: August 2026

1. About this service

Buscando Empleo RD is an automated system that publishes short vertical videos to a single TikTok account (@BuscandoEmpleoRD) announcing job openings in the Dominican Republic. Job listings are collected from public job-alert emails (such as LinkedIn job alerts and Computrabajo alerts), reviewed against automated anti-fraud and duplicate checks, and converted into a video with a narrated summary.

2. Scope of the integration

This app only publishes content to our own TikTok account. It does not offer a login, posting, or any other service on behalf of other TikTok users, and it does not collect or process data belonging to other TikTok accounts.

3. Content and accuracy

Videos contain publicly available job posting information (title, company, location, and a link or contact to apply) plus an automated voice narration. Buscando Empleo RD is not the employer for any listed position and does not guarantee the accuracy, availability, or legitimacy of third-party job postings. Users should verify any offer directly with the listed employer before sharing personal or financial information.

These terms may be updated from time to time. Continued operation of the automation after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
