Privacy Policy — Reels Automation RD

Last updated: November 2026

1. What we collect

The automation processes video and audio from a live broadcast we capture directly, solely to identify and generate short video clips. It does not collect personal data from any individual appearing in the broadcast beyond what is already publicly visible in it.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect personal data from TikTok viewers or followers. We do not use the TikTok API to read, store, or process data about other TikTok accounts. This app does not offer login or any feature on behalf of TikTok users other than the single account it publishes to.

3. Use of the TikTok API

We use the TikTok Content Posting API solely to publish videos to our own TikTok account. Access tokens granted by TikTok are stored securely, used only for this purpose, and are never shared with third parties.

4. Data retention

Generated clips (video, title, caption) are kept only as needed to operate the channel and maintain a record of what was published.
