Privacy Policy — Buscando Empleo RD

Last updated: August 2026

1. What we collect

The automation reads job-alert emails sent to its own dedicated inbox via Gmail's read-only API. It only reads messages from known job-board senders (such as LinkedIn job alerts and Computrabajo) and does not access, read, or store any other email in that inbox.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect personal data from TikTok viewers or followers. We do not use the TikTok API to read, store, or process data about other TikTok accounts. This app does not offer login or any feature on behalf of TikTok users other than the single account it publishes to.

3. Use of the TikTok API

We use the TikTok Content Posting API solely to publish videos to our own TikTok account (@BuscandoEmpleoRD). Access tokens granted by TikTok are stored securely, used only for this purpose, and are never shared with third parties.

4. Data retention

Job posting data (title, company, location, contact/link) and the media generated from it (image, audio, video) are kept only as needed to operate the channel, avoid publishing duplicate listings, and maintain a record of what was published.
